How the fish rating works

Air pressure. Slowly falling pressure before a front is the best sign. A fast rise after a front usually makes fish go quiet.

Moon feeding times. Major times are about 2 hours around when the moon is overhead or underfoot. Minor times are about 1 hour around moonrise and moonset.

Light. Dawn and dusk are best. Cloudy days help. Bright midday sun pushes fish deeper.

Wind, rain and waves. A light breeze and light rain help. Strong wind, storms and rough seas lower the rating and show a safety warning.

Weather, waves, water temperature and tides from Open-Meteo. Moon times are worked out in the page. Always check official marine warnings and tide tables before going out on a boat.
